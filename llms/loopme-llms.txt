# LoopMe

> LoopMe is a global brand-performance advertising platform that uses AI to bring brands into mobile and CTV apps, delivering higher campaign performance for advertisers and premium yield for publishers. Its developer surface consists of a REST Reporting API (publisher and advertiser statistics), a server-to-server (S2S) ad request API, and first-party United SDKs for Android and iOS.

## APIs

- [Reporting API](https://wiki.loopme.cool/publishers/reporting-api): Retrieve app/site and campaign statistics over REST (GET, api_auth_token query param, JSON, USD, UTC).
- [S2S Ad Request API](https://wiki.loopme.cool/publishers/integrations/s2s): Server-to-server ad request endpoint returning ad payloads.
